StudieQuiz is the Android app for the StudieQuiz learning platform: quizzes, results and chat for students and teachers.

With the app you can:

* Sign in with your own StudieQuiz account (e-mail and password, or Google).
* See the quizzes your teacher has published for your class.
* Take a quiz on your phone and get your score straight away.
* Review your answers and talk to your teacher and classmates in the chat.
* As a teacher, open your dashboard to manage classes, quizzes and results.

An account is required. You create one on the website at https://studiequiz.online, and the app uses the same account there.

The app has no ads and no analytics or crash-reporting services. Quiz data is stored on the StudieQuiz server, which is free software in the same repository, but the app cannot be pointed at a different server, so it needs that service to work.

Signing in with Google is optional and only works when Google Play services are installed. Without them you can still sign in with your e-mail address and password.

The app is free and open source, licensed under the GPL-3.0-or-later.
